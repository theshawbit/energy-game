Operator Briefing

How to play

You run a city's power grid in real time, quarter after quarter. A 24-hour clock ticks — demand rises and falls with the daily curve and steps up each quarter. Keep capacity ≥ demand and run the grid as a business.

The loop

  1. Baseload & renewables (nuclear, coal, solar, wind) run automatically. Solar and wind vary with time of day and weather.
  2. Peakers (gas) and the battery are your reserves — commit them as the peak approaches. 123 commit peakers, B fires the battery, or tap a fleet row. They take time to spin up, so pre-commit them.
  3. Manage pauses the clock to queue rolling maintenance, upgrade a plant's four KPI axes (reliability · efficiency · output · flexibility) through its contractor ladders, and commit lead-time builds. A fleet row's ⚙ opens that plant's upgrade ladders.
  4. Each day opens a quick brief (skippable) to set your queues; each quarter closes with a board review that grades you and mints ⚡. Hit Space to pause anytime.

When it goes wrong

Grid strainA shortfall fills the grid-stability reserve (top-right). The deeper the shortfall, the faster it fills; served hours drain it back.
CollapseLet the reserve top out — a sustained shortfall you can't claw back — and the lights go out. The run ends; you take a new posting, keeping your banked ⚡, skills and records.

A shortfall you still have a lever for — an uncommitted plant, a charged battery, a load-shed tier, demand response — fills the reserve slower than one where every lever is already spent. You're never killed while a move remains.

Field questions

Why did a plant trip?
Trips are rare and reserved for neglect. A plant at 85% reliability or better NEVER trips — keep your fleet healthy and you'll almost never see one. Risk only appears below 85% and climbs as reliability decays: ~1%/day at 70%, ~6% at 60%, ~18% at 50%. Queue maintenance before it gets there. One about to go first shows a ⚠ STRAINING warning for a few seconds — commit reserves, shed load, or stand a straining peaker down on your terms.
Why does my reserve margin keep swinging?
The grid is live and volatile — solar clouds over and clears, wind gusts and lulls, and demand wobbles with the occasional short spike (a cold front, an industrial swing). Following that moving net load is the real job: keep a cushion of armable reserves and commit a peaker or the battery when the margin dips. A brief shortfall only fills the strain reserve slowly, so attention beats panic.
What wears a plant out — and how do I fix it?
How hard you run it, and how old it is. A plant leaned on near full load deteriorates far faster than a lightly-loaded reserve, and every plant wears quicker as it ages. Queue rolling maintenance from Manage or the daily brief: a crew works it over a few days (the plant's offline while in the shop), restoring its KPIs toward their caps — a deeper-decayed plant costs more and takes longer. An upgrade (the four KPI ladders) raises the caps maintenance restores toward.
How do I earn?
You sell served power two ways: contracted blocks settle at a price you locked in at the Desk, and the rest clears at the live spot price (the continuous merit-order price). Unserved load earns nothing, and a forced blackout — load you leave dark with no lever to cover it — is penalised on top, so covering the city is always the paying move. Hedge with contracts to tame the swings. The wholesale price up top is what it costs to run plants right now (carbon lifts it for dirty plants).
What's the carbon price?
From day 3 it rises, adding cost to fossil plants and eventually making gas cheaper to run than coal.
Why is my cash falling when nothing's wrong?
Thermal plants (nuclear, coal, gas) have a fixed operating cost each hour, running or not — idle capacity bleeds money, so retire what you don't need in Manage for salvage. Solar and wind are cheap to own. Early on, a passive grid can run a deep cash deficit before its earnings catch up — commit your reserves to serve the peaks and it climbs back.
What carries between postings?
Each quarter's board review mints ⚡ from your profit and the city's satisfaction; spend it on permanent Skills. Your banked ⚡, skills, and best records carry to the next posting. Everything else resets.
