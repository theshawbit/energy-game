How to play

You run a city's power grid in real time. A 24-hour clock ticks by; demand rises and falls with the daily curve and climbs every day you survive. Keep capacity ≥ demand.

The loop

  • Baseload & renewables (nuclear, coal, solar, wind) run automatically. Solar and wind vary with time of day and weather.
  • Peakers (gas) and the battery are your reserves — bring them online as the peak approaches. Keys 1 2 3 arm peakers, B fires the battery, or click a card. Peakers take time to spin up, so pre-arm them.
  • Manage grid pauses the clock to build capacity, upgrade plants (Mk tiers), and service worn plants.
  • Space pauses.

Failing

  • Brownout — a small shortfall: lost sales, fines, falling satisfaction, and a short window to recover.
  • Blackout — a big or sustained shortfall: the run ends.
  • Lost confidence — let satisfaction fall to 0 (chronic brownouts) and the city pulls your licence.

FAQ

  • Why did a plant trip? A well-maintained plant almost never trips — wear is the real driver, so keep plants serviced. When one is about to go, it first shows a ⚠ STRAINING warning for a few seconds: use it to arm reserves or shed load, or stand a straining peaker down on your own terms. Neglected (worn) plants strain far more often.
  • How do I earn? You sell served power two ways: contracted blocks settle at a price you locked in at the Desk, and the rest clears at the live spot price. Spot rises when your grid is tight and crashes when you're flush — so thin-but-covered is lucrative, but a shortfall is never a windfall (unserved load earns nothing). Hedge with contracts to tame the swings. The wholesale price up top is a merit-order signal of what it costs you to run plants right now (carbon lifts it for dirty plants).
  • What's the carbon price? From day 3 it rises, adding cost to fossil plants and eventually making gas cheaper to run than coal.
  • Why is my cash falling when nothing's wrong? Thermal plants (nuclear, coal, gas) have a fixed operating cost each hour, running or not — idle capacity bleeds money, so retire what you don't need in Manage for salvage. Solar and wind are cheap to own.
  • What carries between runs? Meta-currency (⚡) earned at the end of a run buys permanent Skills. Everything else resets.
